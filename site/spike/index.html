A glyph is a view of a loop

Drag the sphere to turn the loop. Let go, and Penrose inks the glyph from that viewpoint.

loop

Momentum and weight shape both views; pressure, the swelling on tight turns, appears in the ink only. k = 1 follows the loop along the sphere's surface. Larger k connects every k-th point of the same loop, so the stroke cuts through the interior, like the star polygon {13/k} on a circle.

Families

Only three closed loops run along the sphere's contact edges (rows A, B, C). Stepping each by k = 1…6 gives 18 distinct glyphs. Click one to load it.
